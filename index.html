How it works

Angel reads the fight. It never touches the game.

The server already tells your game client every hit. Angel listens to that same traffic on your PC and counts it.

SERVER

The game server

Sends your client each hit, heal and kill as the fight happens.

NPCAP

Your network card

Npcap, the free driver Wireshark uses, lets Angel see that traffic. Angel installs it for you.

ANGEL

Counted. Nothing sent.

Packets become damage and DPS. Angel sends nothing and writes nothing to the game.
